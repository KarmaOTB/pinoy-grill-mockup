Our Story

Family-run, fire-cooked, Filipino to the core

Pinoy Grill brings the flavors of Manila's street-food stalls to Chicago — skewers marinated in citrus and soy, noodles stir-fried to order, and the sour, savory, sweet balance that defines Filipino cooking. Everything is cooked fresh to order, ready for pickup or delivery.
